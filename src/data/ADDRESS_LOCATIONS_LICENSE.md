The location data in `addressLocationsData.json` is derived from CountryStateCity v3.0.3. It includes the dataset's available city records for Pakistan, the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, Japan, and Australia.

Source: https://github.com/tansuasici/CountryStateCity

The dataset is licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. This adapted location database is shared under the same license.

Pakistan province, district, and tehsil hierarchy in `pakistanAdministrativeHierarchy.json` is from [Open Admin Data](https://github.com/open-admin-data/pakistan-administrative-divisions) and is licensed under CC-BY-4.0. The final address selector displays tehsils as city/locality choices.
